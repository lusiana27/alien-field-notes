One original line: a review of planet Earth left by an alien tourist, in at
most 200 characters. The entry program writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> Flew 40 light-years for Earth's famous oceans. The hosts made me remove my
> shoes, then charged me rent to stand on their floor. Two stars - the sunset
> was free.

160 characters, plain ASCII, one line. The laugh comes from the alien's
angle on ordinary Earth life, not from a joke bolted onto the premise: the
long journey for the famous oceans, the shoes-off house rule, rent charged
for the very floor you stand on — and a two-star verdict with the punch at
the end: the sunset was free.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Standard library only, no
third-party packages, no external fonts or data files.
